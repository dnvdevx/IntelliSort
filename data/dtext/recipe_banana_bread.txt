Banana Bread Recipe

Ingredients: 3 ripe bananas, 2 cups flour, 1 cup sugar, 2 eggs, 1/2 cup
melted butter, 1 tsp baking soda, pinch of salt.

Instructions: Mash bananas and mix with melted butter. Add sugar, eggs, and
vanilla. Fold in flour, baking soda, and salt. Pour into a loaf pan and
bake at 350F for 60 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.

Simple Tomato Pasta Recipe

Ingredients: 200g pasta, 2 cloves garlic, 1 can crushed tomatoes, olive oil,
basil, salt and pepper.

Instructions: Boil pasta until al dente. Saute garlic in olive oil until
fragrant. Add crushed tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes. Season with salt,
pepper, and fresh basil. Toss with cooked pasta and serve.

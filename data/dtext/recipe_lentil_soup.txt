Hearty Lentil Soup Recipe

Ingredients: 1 cup red lentils, 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 cloves garlic,
vegetable stock, cumin, turmeric, salt.

Instructions: Saute onion, garlic, and carrots until softened. Add lentils,
spices, and stock. Simmer for 25 minutes until lentils are soft. Blend
partially for a creamy texture, then season to taste.
